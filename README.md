# Antonio Mosca

PhD student in Economics, with a BSc and an MSc in Physics. I use methods from statistical physics to study economic and financial systems: network models of markets, collective dynamics and financial instability, and Monte Carlo inference.

### Research

- **Penalized Maximum Likelihood Inference of Core-Periphery Networks**, with Piero Mazzarisi.
  [arXiv:2609.15796](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15796) (2026) · [code](https://github.com/antoniomosca27/core-periphery-us-air-plsd)
  A penalized likelihood correction (PLSD) that lets a maximum-entropy core–periphery model reproduce the wedges and triangles of real cores, applied to interbank and US air traffic networks.

### Projects

- [**financial-crashes-phase-transitions**](https://github.com/antoniomosca27/financial-crashes-phase-transitions): from the Ising model to Kaizoji's interacting investors and Johansen–Ledoit–Sornette crash dynamics, applied to the S&P 500 in 1987, 2008 and 2020.
- [**PIMC-anharmonic-oscillator**](https://github.com/antoniomosca27/PIMC-anharmonic-oscillator): Path Integral Monte Carlo for the quartic anharmonic oscillator, extracting the spectral gap Δ(λ) from Euclidean correlators with bootstrap uncertainties.
- [**QML-qcnn-medmnist**](https://github.com/antoniomosca27/QML-qcnn-medmnist): hybrid quantum–classical convolutional network (Qiskit + PyTorch) for MedMNIST image classification, with Leonardo Tomei.

Every repository comes with automated tests, continuous integration and a reproducible pipeline.
